FAQ

Nine questions we hear every week.

How fast can an AI employee start work?
48 hours for a single AI employee. Six days for the full AI GM stack. We train it on your menu, your POS, and your local rules before it takes its first shift. No IT project, no rewiring, no downtime.
How does the 3x ROI guarantee actually work?
On the discovery call we agree in writing on the metrics we will move. Recovered call revenue, labor hours saved, waste reduction, whatever fits your restaurant. If, after 90 days, the tracked lift is less than 3x the monthly lease, we work free until it is. This is in the contract, not a marketing line.
Do I need to be technical to run this?
No. The point of leasing an AI employee is that you supervise, not operate. You get a dashboard, weekly numbers, and a human on our side who handles every technical thing. You focus on the food, the guests, and the bottom line.
What if I use an old POS or paper spreadsheets?
That is fine. Most Ontario restaurants we work with are still on legacy POS systems or paper. The AI reads menus, printed order pads, PDF invoices, and phone recordings just as well as it reads structured data. We meet you where you are.
How do you protect my data and customer info?
My background is DevSecOps for banks. Every AI employee runs with least-privilege access, encrypted data in transit and at rest, and Canadian data residency by default. Your customer phone numbers, orders, and financials are yours. If you cancel, we return everything and delete our copies.
What happens if the AI does something wrong on a call?
Every call is recorded, transcribed, and reviewed. If the AI mishandles something, we hear it, retrain the model, and follow up with the guest ourselves. That is what supervision means. You do not have to be the one catching mistakes.
Do my current staff lose their jobs?
Almost never. Restaurants that lease an AI GM usually keep the same headcount but move people from the phone and clipboard to the tables and the kitchen. The AI takes the tedious work. Your team does the hospitality work.
What if I want to cancel?
30 days notice, any time, any reason. No long-term contract, no exit fee. If you cancel, we turn off the AI employee, return your data, and delete our copies within 14 days.
What if my restaurant is not in Ontario?
Ontario is where we operate today. It is where we know the local rules, tax logic, and food safety requirements. If you are outside Ontario, we can still talk. Priority goes to restaurants in Toronto, Ottawa, Hamilton, and the GTA.
