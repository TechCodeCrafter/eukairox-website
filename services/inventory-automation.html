What We Build

Your Inventory, Always Accurate

Every system is custom-built around your sales channels, your warehouse flow, and your supply chain.

Real-Time Inventory Sync

Stock levels stay in sync across every sales channel in real time. When a product sells on your website, the count updates on your marketplace listings and in your warehouse system simultaneously. No overselling, no confused customers, no frantic manual adjustments after the fact.

Automated Reorder Alerts

The system monitors sales velocity and lead times for every product. When stock drops below a threshold based on how fast it is selling and how long it takes to restock, you get alerted automatically. Purchase orders can even be pre-drafted so all you do is approve and send. You reorder at exactly the right time, every time.

Stock Level Dashboards

Live dashboards give you instant visibility into stock levels across every location and channel. See which products are running low, which are overstocked, and where your inventory is sitting geographically. Your operations team makes decisions from real data instead of best guesses and outdated spreadsheets.

Cross-Platform Reconciliation

When your inventory data lives in multiple systems, discrepancies are inevitable. Our automation reconciles data across all platforms continuously. Mismatches get flagged and corrected. Your numbers always agree, and you never ship a product you do not actually have in stock.

Demand Forecasting

AI analyzes your historical sales patterns, seasonal trends, and market signals to predict future demand. You know which products will need restocking next month and which ones are slowing down. Purchasing decisions are based on data, not gut feelings. Cash stays invested in the products that actually move.
