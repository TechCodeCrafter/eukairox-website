The Problem

What Is Costing You Placements

These are the bottlenecks that slow down every recruitment agency we work with. Speed wins in this business, and these problems are making you slower.

Too many resumes and not enough time to screen them. Your recruiters are spending hours reviewing applications when they should be having conversations with the best candidates.

Slow candidate response times are losing you placements. The best candidates get multiple offers, and if your response takes a day instead of an hour, they are already talking to another agency.

Manual ATS updates after every call and email. Your recruiters are spending 30 minutes after each interaction typing notes and updating records instead of moving to the next candidate.

Follow-up sequences falling through the cracks. Candidates and clients go cold because no one remembered to send the next email or make the next call on time.

Phone screening is eating recruiter hours that should be spent closing. Initial qualification calls are repetitive and predictable, but your best people are doing them instead of building relationships.

What We Automate

Your Recruiting Pipeline, Fully Automated

Every system is custom-built around your agency's workflow, your roles, and your ATS.

AI Voice Agents for Pre-Screening

AI voice agents call candidates and run initial qualification screens automatically. They ask the right questions, capture responses, and score candidates based on your criteria. Your recruiters get a qualified shortlist without spending an hour on the phone with every applicant.

Lead Qualification and Routing

Incoming candidates and client leads get scored and routed to the right recruiter instantly. Hot candidates go to the top of the queue. Client inquiries get matched with the recruiter who handles that industry or role type. No manual sorting, no missed opportunities.

Automated Follow-Up Sequences

Candidates and clients get follow-up messages on a schedule that matches their stage in the process. No one falls through the cracks. The system sends check-ins, reminders, and status updates automatically while keeping your recruiters in the loop on every conversation.

ATS Sync

Every call, email, and interaction gets logged in your ATS automatically. Candidate status updates, notes from AI screens, recruiter activity. Your system is always up to date without anyone typing a single note manually after a call.

Interview Scheduling

Qualified candidates get booked directly into recruiter and hiring manager calendars. The system checks availability, sends confirmations, handles rescheduling, and sends reminders before the call. Your recruiters stop playing calendar tennis and start closing more placements.
